The global AI landscape is moving faster than ever. Every single day, there is a new model release, a new funding round, or a new regulation.

But as an Indian developer or founder, your time is limited. You don't need to know what a VC in San Francisco had for breakfast. You need to know what matters for **your** work in **your** market.

That is why we built **PulseAI**. 

Every morning, our AI engine scans the best sources in the world (OpenAI, Anthropic, The Verge) and writes a custom "Why it matters" summary specifically for the Indian context.

**What's next?**
This blog is where we will publish deeper, original analysis that doesn't fit in a daily feed. Expect articles on:
- AI policy changes in India.
- Deep dives into new model pricing.
- Interviews with Indian AI founders.

Thanks for reading. See you in your inbox.
